General Election Ballots Returned: October 30, 2026

Denver, October 30, 2026 - The Secretary of State’s office will send a daily update every weekday through Thursday, November 12 reporting the number of ballots returned and processed by County Clerks for the 2026 General Election. So far, 1,335,298 ballots have been returned statewide.
